Key takeaways

Size drives the total, not affluence. Population correlates with total e-waste at r≈0.76, but per-capita generation correlates with population at r≈−0.45 — bigger countries generate more overall, but less per person.
Room to grow. India's actual output is ~4,300M kg below its population-predicted level — the largest gap of any country — pointing to sharp volume growth as incomes and device ownership rise.
Framework ahead of peers. Brazil, Indonesia, Mexico, Egypt, Nigeria and South Africa all have legislation but lack collection/recycling targets. India already has both.

Limitations: single-year snapshot (2022), 20 countries only. Recycling volumes, recycling rates, and GDP were 100% missing in the source file and are not shown here.
